FULL UTILIZATION OF CURRENT FUNCTIONALITY
=========================================

Date: 2026-08-23
Project: Budget Annihilation / rts-prototype


PURPOSE
========

The engine supports more gameplay than the roster currently communicates. The
goal is not a BAR-sized unit count. The goal is a compact roster in which every
important movement medium, information tier, weapon family, counter, economy
system, and work command has a clear host and a deliberate showcase.

Current scale: 27 units, 21 buildings, 11 travelling-shot blueprints, four
continuous-beam rays, five shield blueprints, and 72 turret configurations.
The main imbalance is many ground direct-fire/capstone units versus few
intelligence, support, naval-surface, anti-sub, construction, and counter units.


DECISIONS
=========

1. Keep the Universal Fabricator
--------------------------------

Production should have five Fabricators at T1 and the same five at T2:

T1:

* Universal Fabricator
* Bot Fabricator
* Vehicle Fabricator
* Aircraft Fabricator
* Naval Fabricator

T2:

* Advanced Universal Fabricator
* Advanced Bot Fabricator
* Advanced Vehicle Fabricator
* Advanced Aircraft Fabricator
* Advanced Naval Fabricator

The T1 Universal roster is the deduplicated union of all four T1 specialist
rosters. The Advanced Universal roster is the deduplicated union of all four T2
specialist rosters. Neither Universal may produce units from the other tier.

Target about five menu entries per specialist Fabricator per tier, with equal
counts across Bot, Vehicle, Aircraft, and Naval. This is 20 specialist menu
entries per tier, not necessarily 20 unique units: cross-domain units count in
each appropriate specialist roster.

Examples:

* Hippo: Vehicle + Naval.
* Duck: Aircraft + Naval.
* Water Strider: Bot + Naval.
* Torpedo gunship: Aircraft + Naval.
* Pure tanks: Vehicle only.
* Pure bots/crawlers: Bot only.
* Pure planes: Aircraft only.
* Surface ships and submarines: Naval only.

Data rule: every unit should author one tech level and one or more production
domains. Specialist menus are derived from those fields; Universal menus are
derived from the four same-tier specialist sets. Validate this so overlap never
creates duplicate Universal entries and roster changes cannot drift.

Cost rule:

* Start all four specialists at a tier with comparable cost and build power.
* A same-tier Universal initially costs about 3x a specialist in energy, metal,
  and build time.
* The Universal pays for flexibility, not throughput. It should remain near a
  specialist in build power, durability, and footprint efficiency.
* T2 has its own higher specialist baseline; Advanced Universal is 3x that T2
  baseline, not 3x the T1 Universal.

Placement and access:

* Preserve ground-or-water placement for both Universal tiers.
* Naval Fabricators must support water placement.
* Bot and Vehicle Fabricators default to ground placement.
* Aircraft Fabricator can have the "building hover" type, where they are built anywhere.
* Advanced Fabricators require a construction craft of that T1 level to create it. Any construction bot can continue building something, but an advanced fabricator can only be started by the T1 construction craft of that type. 

Let's have the "t1 universal fabricator" create any t1 construction craft and a "t2 universal fabricator" create any t2 construction craft.

A t1 construction craft can create a t1 or t2 fabricator of its type and can create a t1 or t2 universal fabricator (again 3x more expensive).





2. Use a Water Strider for amphibious-crawler coverage
-----------------------------------------------------

The unused amphibious-crawler idea should become an insect-like Water Strider:
a crawler that walks on land and skims across the water surface. Use the current
force model with strong upward support from both water-related surface-following
lift channels. It should stop and hold position rather than fly like a plane.

This is a better demonstration of the physics architecture than a conventional
hovercraft because its behavior visibly comes from forces, support, water
contact, momentum, and arrival control. Put it in both Vehicle and Naval
Fabricators at its assigned tier.

the "ampbibious tank" thing should be an under-water seabed type movement (I think it is now) and the "ampbibious-crawler" is an "above water walking/crawling" type.


3. Keep transports single-passenger and visible
-----------------------------------------------

All transports should carry exactly one unit. Generalize transport identity,
allowed cargo, and tuning if needed, but do not add multi-unit capacity.

The passenger must never disappear into hidden cargo state. It remains visibly
attached and targetable while carried. A transporter can carry any unit, friend or foe. A passenger can also attack its transport if for example it is on an opposing team.


4. Keep sensing and attacking independent
-----------------------------------------

A unit being able to detect a target must not imply that it can attack it, and
a weapon being able to reach a medium must not imply that its host can detect
that medium alone.

Examples:

* Hippo and Sea Turtle may sense underwater targets without receiving an
  underwater weapon.
* A Petrel torpedo gunship may attack submarines but should still benefit from
  allied sonar instead of having every intelligence tool built in.
* Long-range artillery may consume team radar/sonar knowledge without owning a
  long-range sensor.

Preserve separate sensor matrices, weapon trajectory matrices, target masks,
and team intelligence. This separation is one of the project's strongest
features.


5. Remove unused pulsed-laser and cylindrical-shield branches
------------------------------------------------------------

Do not add units merely to justify dormant functionality.

* Delete the unused pulsed-laser content/path instead of adding a Pulse Laser
  unit or tower. Keep the four existing continuous-beam families.
* Delete the unused infinite cylindrical and aimed-cylindrical shield
  blueprints/path instead of adding a Shield Picket.
* Keep the shield shapes that already have meaningful hosts: Daddy/Widow
  spheres and Loris's aimed reflective panel.
* Remove associated dead configuration and tests when implementation work is
  scheduled, while preserving shared code still used by beams or existing
  shields.

  The things we are removing (above) are either redundant or hurt performance. 


HIGHEST-VALUE ROSTER GAPS
=========================

Priority A: expose systems that mostly exist now
------------------------------------------------

1. Mobile radar scout/AWACS

Add a dedicated, lightly armed or unarmed mobile radar unit. Do not assume the
Bee must be repurposed. This makes contact-only information, mobile spotting,
radar stealth, jamming, AA escort, and contact-fire artillery routine gameplay.

2. Ground constructor

Add a cheap ground builder with a limited T1 build menu. The Commander should
not be the only ground host for build, repair, reclaim, guard, and assist.

3. Resurrection bot and resurrection submarine

Let's remove resurrection capabilities now - it's too complex. Currently units just explode and that's the end of it. 

4. Cloaked scout, detector, and mobile jammer

Add three narrow roles rather than another all-purpose super-unit:

* fragile cloak/radar-stealth scout;
* detector with modest ordinary sight but no radar;
* mobile radar jammer with weak combat capability.

Together with Mongoose radar stealth, Orca sonar stealth, Duck's sonar jammer,
Radar, Sonar, and the static jammers, these form a complete information-counter
loop.

5. Mobile ground AA

Add an affordable air-only missile rover. Current mobile AA is effectively an
Eagle fighter or one subsystem on Rex; neither provides ordinary ground-army
air defense.

6. Dedicated point defense

Add an Interceptor Tower that attacks targetable rockets and mortar shells.
Show projectile health and interception deliberately rather than leaving the
behavior incidental on general AA/beam turrets.

7. Dumb-rocket unit

Add the requested straight, never-turning rocket beside the current controlled
rocket. The contrast demonstrates aim, target motion, guidance, collision, and
point defense with existing projectile machinery.

8. Cluster artillery or bomber

Enable the existing submunition path on one readable weapon. The heavy mortar
and submunition configuration are otherwise unused. Do not add another beam or
ordinary plasma unit first.

9. Tidal/Floating Generator

Add a water-placeable energy producer so a naval expansion can close its own
economy loop with seabed extraction, energy, Sonar, defense, and production.


Priority B: complete movement-medium coverage
---------------------------------------------

10. Water Strider

Implement the amphibious-crawler described above. It should be a visible
force/medium showcase and appear in both Vehicle and Naval rosters.

11. Patrol Corvette

Add a true surface vessel with an above-water gun and sonar. A submerged mount
or separate anti-sub weapon is optional; sensing does not require attacking.
This provides the missing partially submerged surface hull.

12. Petrel torpedo gunship/aerosub

Add an anti-sub aircraft. The current Torpedo is underwater-launch only, so it
needs a small new air-launch/enter-water locomotion profile built from the
existing medium-transition and guidance machinery.

13. Naval AA or jammer vessel

Add only if the Corvette cannot carry one of these roles without becoming
unclear. Prefer a specialist over another multi-weapon capstone.


WHAT IS OVER-REPRESENTED 
========================

* Ground direct-fire bodies, especially crawler/beam variants.
* Giant units that combine weapons, detection, shields, and/or production. Rex,
  the Queens, and Widow are impressive but poor teachers of individual systems.
* Continuous beams and ordinary above-water plasma relative to dumb rockets,
  clusters, point defense, and underwater counters.
* Static intelligence relative to mobile radar, detectors, and jammers.
* Demo factories: one Universal Fabricator per unit line can produce 26 factory
  buildings per player. The problem is this duplication, not Universal itself.

Before increasing total roster size substantially, rework curent roster into t1 and t2 splits, and rework costs and firepower etc. 

- Let's have "loris" and "daddy" be t1, "widow" be t2


BAR COMPARISON: IMPORTANT LESSONS
=================================

BAR is useful as a coverage check, not as a target unit count.

* Production domains: bots, vehicles, aircraft, ships, hovercraft,
  amphibious units, and seaplanes create readable strategic identities. The
  proposed specialist-plus-premium-Universal matrix captures this while
  permitting overlap.
* Naval ecosystem: surface combat, mobile sonar/jamming, AA, anti-sub,
  construction, resurrection, and water economy matter more than many tiers of
  battleships.
* Air ecosystem: radar scouts, fighters, bombers/gunships, torpedo aircraft,
  transports, constructors, and mobile/static AA create counters. The project
  already has distinct plane and drone movement; it needs connective roles.
* Intelligence: sight, contacts, stealth, cloak, jamming, and detection should
  have separate hosts and counters.
* Battlefield lifecycle: dedicated repair/reclaim/resurrection units make
  wrecks and salvage ordinary strategy rather than Commander-only novelty.

Useful later BAR-style systems that require genuinely new engine work:

* EMP/paralyze and other timed status effects;
* fire/napalm and persistent damage areas;
* mines, walls, and perimeter cameras;
* strategic stockpiles, manual missiles, nukes, and anti-nuke interception;
* richer radar uncertainty/terrain occlusion;
* energy-consuming cloak and automatic decloak rules;
* deeper tech/economy escalation.

Do not begin those by disguising ordinary damage or cooldown weapons. First
finish the counters supported by current systems.
